Our Process

How a Tile Project Runs

1

Free On-Site Estimate & Tile Matching

We assess the scope in person, and if it's a repair, start tracking down tile that matches what's already there.

2

Careful Removal & Substrate Prep

Damaged or dated tile is removed without disturbing what's staying, and the substrate underneath is inspected and repaired before anything new goes down.

3

Waterproofing & Layout Planning

Modern waterproofing membranes go in on wet areas, and the layout is dry-fit and planned so cuts and pattern lines land where they should.

4

Setting, Cutting & Grouting

Tile is set, leveled, and cut to fit around fixtures and niches, then grouted with tight, consistent lines once the adhesive has cured.

5

Walkthrough & Cleanup

We walk the finished space with you, seal grout where needed, and leave the area as clean as we found it.

Repair or Replace

We'll Tell You Straight

Not every tile problem needs a full re-tile, and not every re-tile can be avoided — we'll give you an honest read on which one actually fits your situation.

When a Repair Is Enough

A cracked or chipped tile here and there, a section damaged by a dropped tool, or a few tiles that need to come out for plumbing access — these are usually straightforward spot repairs, matched to the surrounding tile.

When a Full Re-Tile Makes Sense

Widespread water damage, failing waterproofing underneath, or wanting a completely different layout or material are all good reasons to go with a full re-tile instead of patching around the problem.

Prefer to keep your existing tile and just refresh how it looks? See our Ceramic Tile Refinishing page for an in-place alternative to replacement.
