Our Process

How We Refinish a Sink or Vanity Top

Every sink we refinish goes through the same careful process, whether it's a standalone porcelain sink or an integrated cultured marble sink-and-counter unit.

1

Protect & Prep the Space

We mask off the surrounding counter, cabinetry, walls, and floor with plastic sheeting and tape so overspray and dust stay contained to the sink itself.

2

Remove Faucet & Drain Hardware

The faucet, drain, and pop-up assembly are carefully disconnected and set aside so the whole surface can be coated edge to edge.

3

Repair Chips, Cracks & Wear

Chips and cracks — especially around the drain and rim — are filled and repaired before any coating goes on, and given time to fully cure.

4

Strip, Etch & Sand

The existing surface is stripped of soap scum and old coating, then chemically etched and hand-sanded to create a profile the new coating can bond to.

5

Prime & Spray the Coating

We spray a bonding primer followed by multiple coats of a high-gloss finish, color matched to your existing vanity or counter.

6

Cure, Reinstall & Walkthrough

Once the final coat is on, the faucet and drain hardware are reinstalled. We walk the finished sink with you and confirm exactly when it's safe to use.

The reason sink refinishing works so well is that it solves the actual problem — a worn, stained, or outdated surface — without touching everything around it. Before we spray anything, we take the guesswork out of matching your existing vanity top, countertop, or surround, so the refinished sink looks like it was never anything but original to the room.

Care & Maintenance

Keeping a Refinished Sink Looking New

A refinished sink is durable, but a little care in the first few weeks makes a big difference over the long run.

  • Wait before use. Give the coating its full cure window — usually 24 to 48 hours — before running water in the sink.
  • Skip abrasive cleaners. Scouring powders, steel wool, and harsh abrasive pads will dull a refinished surface over time. Stick to non-abrasive liquid cleaners and a soft cloth or sponge.
  • Rinse away hair dye, toothpaste & harsh chemicals promptly. Left sitting, these can stain or dull the finish over time.
  • Use a soft tray under bottles & accessories. A rubber tray or basket keeps bottles and containers from scratching the surface directly.
  • Avoid dropping heavy or sharp objects. The coating resists everyday wear well, but a dropped bottle or razor can still chip it, just like it could a factory finish.
