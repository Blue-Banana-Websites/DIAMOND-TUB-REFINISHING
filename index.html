Cultured Marble Refinishing & Repairs

Cultured marble tubs, sinks, vanities, and showers are built to last — but the glossy gel coat that gives them their shine doesn't last forever. Heat, hard water, and years of cleaning can leave it yellowed, dull, cracked, or chipped.

This month we're focused on bringing cultured marble surfaces back to life: repairing thermal-shock cracks and chips, restoring a yellowed or dulled gel coat, and refinishing the whole piece to a smooth, factory-fresh shine — all without tearing out the fixture. Refinishing runs a fraction of replacement cost and can add years of life to a surface that's otherwise still structurally sound.

  • Cracks & chips repaired
  • Yellowed gel coat restored
  • Tubs, sinks, vanities & showers
