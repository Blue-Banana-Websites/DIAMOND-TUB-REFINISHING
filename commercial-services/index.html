Our Process

How a Commercial Project Runs

Whether it's one unit or twenty, every commercial job follows the same reliable process — scoped up front and delivered on schedule.

1

Free On-Site Estimate

A technician walks the units or property with you and provides a no-obligation quote, scoped to exactly what needs repair or refinishing.

2

Scheduling That Works Around You

We batch multiple units into one visit or coordinate around guest stays, tenant turnover, or a builder's possession date.

3

Protect & Prep Each Space

The same masking and surface prep as any residential job, done unit by unit so surrounding floors, walls, and fixtures stay clean.

4

Repair, Strip & Refinish

Chips, cracks, and wear are repaired first, then the surface is stripped, etched, and rebuilt with a durable, high-gloss coating.

5

Walkthrough & Sign-Off

Each unit is reviewed and confirmed ready before we move to the next, so nothing gets missed on a multi-unit job.
